# Finger-loop down indicator — assembly steps

**Draft, started 2026-10-03. Not complete.** The repository has no record of
this part being printed or assembled. Where a step is not known it reads
**unknown**. Nothing here is guessed. Parts are in [PARTS.md](PARTS.md).

## What the model tells us

- The cord passes through two 7.0mm holes in the body.
- At each hole, a 2.5mm groove leads away to one side on the watch face and
  to the opposite side on the wrist side.
- The cord named by the owner is 3.2mm, which is wider than the 2.5mm groove.
- The strap passes between the lugs at each end of the body. The gap is
  20.2mm.

## Open tests the design calls for

Recorded in the design documents and not yet done, as far as the repository
shows:

- Whether the groove holds a real cord.
- Whether the lugs are strong enough on a wrist.
- Whether the 1mm edge chamfer is comfortable on the wrist side.
- Whether the bed-side groove prints smooth enough not to drag the cord.
