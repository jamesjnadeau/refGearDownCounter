Repair guides

How to take each counter apart, change a part, and put it back together. The design files are published, so every part can be printed again.

These guides are drafts. They are written from the design files and from what the designer has told us. Apart from what the designer has told us, such as how to open a counter, nobody has yet followed them on a printed counter. Every step that still needs that check is marked, and every fact we do not have is marked too. Nothing here is guessed.

How to read the marks

MarkWhat it means
From the filesMeasured on the published design files. No printed counter was used.
From the designerThe designer told us. The designer has used these counters in games.
Not checked on a printed unitThe step follows from the shape of the parts, but nobody has done it on a real counter and written down what happened.
Not recordedWe do not have this fact yet. The guide does not fill the gap with a guess.

Before any repair

  • Take a photo of the counter before you take anything out. Where a guide says a detail is not recorded, your own counter is the record: copy what you find.
  • If a step needs force and the guide does not say how much, stop. That step has not been checked yet.
  • Work over a tray. The magnets are 6 mm across and easy to lose.
